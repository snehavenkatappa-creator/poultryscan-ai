🚨 Notifiable Disease Protocol

If your flock exhibits signs of acute velogenic Newcastle Disease or Avian Influenza (sudden massive mortality, cyanotic combs, torticollis, dyspnea):

  • Immediately isolate the affected shed.
  • Enforce strict farm gate lockdown (no bird or litter movement).
  • Notify local government agricultural and veterinary disease control authorities.
