How Flock Analysis Works:
  • Detection: FasterRCNN detector identifies individual visible chickens and assigns unique scan IDs.
  • Classification: Each crop is processed by the MobileNetV2 body classifier using exact 224×224 normalization.
  • Quality Check: Distant, small (<50px), or ambiguous crops are automatically tagged "Uncertain — manual review required".
